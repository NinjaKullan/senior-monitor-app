The holiday visit: you can just visit. Go home to be together, not to run a list of checks.

A lot of holiday advice is a list of things to check on your parents when you visit.

I'd skip the list. Go home, eat the meal, play the game, listen to the stories.

Your parents are adults with their own plans. A visit can just be a visit.
