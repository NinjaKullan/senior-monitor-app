Patrick Hand, ink, centred on paper, never over a face or a screen's text.

App screens are real recordings of the Rehearsal family, renamed on screen to "Mom" and "Dad".

## Self-check (brief §5 and the writers' brief), run on every line

- Banned words (monitor, track, alert, alarm, surveillance, elderly, senior, sensor, detect,
  dashboard, score, safe, fine, okay as a verdict, checked in, ordinary, signals, pings,
  shortcuts, automation, API, MCP, "your loved one"): none in any line.
- No verdict, count, statistic, customer or quote. The two notes in shot 8 are the brief's own examples, shown as Rehearsal notes.
- Straight apostrophes, no em dashes, no exclamation marks.
- "Mom" and "Dad" throughout; "heard from", never "checked in"; "normal" appears only in the app's own note.
- One call to action: heykettle.com, with "Now open to founding families."
- Parents capable and busy; no faces, no hospital, no sad beats; the child is never shown anxious.

## Lines I was unsure of

1. Shot 3, "iPhone or Android": in on the founder's ruling (overrides brief §4). `docs/baton.md`
   still says not to hand an Android family a link until the setup page's Android branch lands.
   Worth checking the claim is true on release day.
2. Shot 5, "And when Kettle last heard from her." starts with "And" and leans on shot 4. It reads
   well in sequence but not on its own (the 9:16 cut keeps the pair together).
3. Shot 9, "Who to call, if you can't reach her." is the app's own tab name. It's the one line
   that touches worry. It stays calm because the screen shows a tidy list, not a situation.
4. Shot 14, Care Compare: RULED, home health agencies instead of nursing homes.
5. Shot 11a, "Kettle works inside Claude, too." The brief allows "connector for Claude, ChatGPT and
   other assistants". I named only Claude, because ChatGPT's listing isn't accepted yet and the
   recording is in Claude.
6. Left out to keep under 90 s: two circles, invite by email, pause, household devices, the story
   behind the name, the price. Any of these can replace shot 10 if you'd rather.
