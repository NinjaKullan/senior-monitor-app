# Kettle launch video: voice-over

Approved by the founder on 2026-09-26, ready to record. The founder allowed an AI voice-over the
same day, overriding the brief's "do not synthesize a voice". It is generated by the founder in
ElevenLabs with a stock voice, with no cloning.

The rules each line follows:
- Plain spoken English: calm, short sentences.
- The same facts as the captions and nothing new. Each line says what its caption says, or less,
  in speaking words.
- Every law in brief §5. The lines are run through the same copy check as the captions
  (`shots.check`).
- About 130 words a minute, each line inside its shot.
- Silence where the screen does the work: Claude typing and looking things up (11a, 14a).

Times are for the cut as it will be once the voice goes in. Shot 1 grows by 0.5 s (2.3 to 2.8 s)
for the full line "Mom and Dad live far away.", and shot 15b by 0.5 s (2.3 to 2.8 s) for the
sign-off. Both are inside the founder's plus or minus 0.5 s, and the film goes from 46.6 to 47.6 s.

The sign-off runs 0.4 s past 15b at 130 wpm. That is the length of 15c's caption fade-in, so its
last word ends while "Now open to founding families." is still appearing, before it can be read.
"Kettle." lands as the name fades in. A take of 2.8 s or less avoids the overlap entirely.

## Recording it in ElevenLabs

- **Voice:** a stock voice from the library, calm, warm and unhurried, in the same voice for every
  line. No cloning.
- **Model:** the most natural English text-to-speech model on the account.
- **Voice settings:**
  - Stability 0.60, so the reading stays even from line to line.
  - Similarity 0.75, the default.
  - Style 0.05, which leaves almost no dramatic colouring.
  - Speaker boost on.
  - Speed 1.0 to begin with. Lower it to 0.95 if a take sounds rushed, or raise it to 1.05 if the
    sign-off runs over 2.8 s.
- **Text:** the 14 spoken lines above, in order, one per paragraph, with a pause between them. Use
  `<break time="1.0s" />` where the editor supports it, or a blank line. Nothing is read for the
  silent shots.
- **Pronunciation:** say "Kettle" plainly, stress on the first syllable, and "Alexa" is not read
  (the caption carries it). "Medicare's" is standard.
- **Export:** MP3, 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps or better, saved as `audio/voiceover.mp3`. Alternatively, one
  file per line named by shot (`audio/vo/04.mp3`) skips the splitting.

Once the file is in, I split it on the pauses, place each line at its shot's start, and set shots
01 and 15b to 2.8 s. I nudge any other shot by up to 0.5 s where a take runs long, duck the music
under the voice if a music file is present, normalize the mix to -16 LUFS, and rebuild both cuts.
