# Kettle launch video: voice-over (draft for the founder's review)

Timed to the 46.6 s cut in `shots.py`. The founder allowed an AI voice-over on 2026-09-26, overriding
the brief's "do not synthesize a voice". It is generated by the founder in ElevenLabs with a stock
voice, with no cloning.

The rules each line follows:
- Plain spoken English: calm, short sentences.
- The same facts as the captions and nothing new. Each line says what its caption says, or less,
  in speaking words.
- Every law in brief §5. The lines are run through the same copy check as the captions
  (`shots.check`): no banned words, no verdicts, no em dashes, "heard from" rather than
  "checked in", Mom and Dad.
- About 130 words a minute. Each line fits inside its shot at that pace, so the voice never runs
  into the next caption.
- Silence where the screen does the work: Claude typing and looking things up (11a, 14a) and the
  close (15b, 15c).

## Lines I was unsure of

1. **Shot 10, "Everyone sees the same notes."** The caption says "brothers and sisters". "Everyone"
   means the same people in fewer words, but it is broader. The caption's own words run 3.2 s
   against a 3.1 s shot.
2. **Shot 9, "Who to call, in one place."** "In one place" comes from the brief ("one screen, no
   hunting through group chats"), not from the caption. It is true, but it is the only line that
   adds a phrase of its own.
3. **Shot 10b, "Add her smart plug, too."** It leaves out Alexa, and the caption carries it.
4. **The close is silent.** A closing line such as "Kettle. For checking in, not checking up."
   would fit the 4.3 s if you'd like the voice to sign off.

## Generating it

- A stock ElevenLabs voice: calm, warm and unhurried, with no accent or character play.
  Stability around 0.6 and style low, so the reading stays even.
- One file, `audio/voiceover.mp3`. The lines are read in the order above, with about a second of
  silence between them and nothing for the silent shots. I split it on the pauses, place each
  line at its shot's start, and nudge shot lengths by up to 0.5 s where a take runs long.
- Alternatively, one file per line, named by shot (`audio/vo/04.mp3`), which skips the splitting.
