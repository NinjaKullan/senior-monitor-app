v6 (as v5) uses one take, `audio/voiceover-v3.mp3` (37.5 s, mono, supplied by the founder on 2026-09-28; a
stock ElevenLabs voice, no cloning). The founder allowed an AI voice-over on 2026-09-26 (brief,
section 8). No older voice file is used.

**How the take was split:** the take has 17 pauses of 0.25 s or longer, at 40 dB below full scale.
The ten lines are the founder's text, in order. Each boundary between lines is a pause after a
full stop, and every pause inside a line falls at a comma or full stop of that line. Each line's
length also fits its word count: 2.2 to 4.0 words a second, pauses included, and slowest where the
line holds the most pauses. Each line is cut 0.03 s before
its first sound and 0.06 s after its last, and kept whole, natural pauses included.

**Placement (`shots.VOICE`):** each line starts 0.2 s after its shot appears, with at
least 0.5 s of quiet between lines.

## Mix

- **Sounds:** a soft tick and a light paper pop in shot 5 (SCRIPT.md, Sounds), in both versions.

- **Music:** `audio/music.mp3` from 0 s at one steady level, 18 dB below the voice's speaking
  level, with no swells in the gaps; in over 0.5 s, out over the last 2 s.
- **Voice-only version:** the same without the music.
- **Loudness:** both at -16 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP or lower.
