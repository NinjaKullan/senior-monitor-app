# Kettle strip: style bible and casts

This text is pasted into every image prompt. Keep it short and literal. The lettering
(speech bubbles, captions, signature) is added by tools/social/make_strip.py, never by
the image model.

## Style
Classic newspaper comic strip. Clean confident ink line, flat colour, no gradients, no
photo textures, no 3D. Palette only: warm paper #F7F1E8 background, warm ink #403C36 lines,
one accent green #297A5C (used sparingly: the kettle, a scarf, a mug), pale grey-green for
shadow. No true black or white. Faces are simple: dot eyes, one-line mouths, expressive
eyebrows. Understated humour; the feeling is relief, never alarm.

NO TEXT of any kind in the image: no speech bubbles, no captions, no signs, no labels, no
letters on objects. Keep the top third of every panel plain and uncluttered (wall, sky,
ceiling, or soft background) so a speech bubble can be placed there afterwards.

## Cast A (even ISO weeks): SAM and JUNE
SAM, the adult child, about 45. Medium build, short dark hair with a little grey at the
temples, round glasses, a plain crew-neck sweater with rolled sleeves. Lives far away;
usually seen at a kitchen counter, a desk, or on a train, phone in hand. Warm, a bit
rumpled, thinks a lot.

JUNE, the parent, about 74. Slim, upright, silver hair in a short practical cut, cardigan
over a collared shirt, reading glasses pushed up on her head. Capable and busy: gardening
gloves, a crossword, a library book, a watering can. Her iPhone is an ordinary one that
sits on the kitchen table or in a cardigan pocket. She is never frail, confused, or
waiting by a window.

## Cast B (odd ISO weeks): DANA and WALT
DANA, the adult child, about 50. Tall, curly shoulder-length hair tied back, small hoop
earrings, a denim shirt over a t-shirt. Lives far away; usually seen in a home office,
on a bus, or walking with a phone. Brisk, funny, organised.

WALT, the parent, about 78. Stocky, bald with a neat white beard, thick-framed glasses, a
flannel shirt and a wristwatch he still winds. Capable and busy: a workbench, a bicycle he
maintains, the newspaper at the diner counter, a chess set. His iPhone lives in his shirt
pocket. He is never frail, confused, or waiting by a window.

## Always
THE KETTLE, a small stovetop kettle in accent green with a curved spout. It appears
somewhere in most strips as a quiet background object. It never has a face, never talks,
never glows or beeps.

## What must never appear
Cameras, screens full of charts, graphs, meters, rings, scores, red lights, alarms, sirens,
hospital or medical items, pill bottles, walkers, wheelchairs, nurses, worried
hand-wringing, a parent slumped or lost. No app screens at all (only a plain phone, face
down or showing a blank lit screen). No real people, no logos.

## Lettering rules (for the words the script places)
Straight apostrophes only. No em dashes. The word "normal", never "ordinary". Never these
words: monitor, monitoring, track, tracking, alert, alerts, surveillance, elderly, seniors,
senior citizens, aging parents, dashboard, sensor, detect, safety device. Twelve words or
fewer per bubble, twelve or fewer per caption.
