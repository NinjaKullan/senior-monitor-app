Kettle in your assistant

If you already talk to Claude or ChatGPT, you can ask it about Mom or Dad the way you would ask a sibling: how her day is going, what the family has written this week, who to call if you can’t reach her. You can add a note by saying it. Everyone in your family circle sees the same record, whichever assistant they use.

Who can connect

Anyone who is a member of a Kettle family circle. Each person connects their own assistant with their own Kettle sign-in, and sees only the circles they belong to.

How to connect

In your assistant’s settings, add a connector and give it this address:

https://api.heykettle.com/mcp

Your assistant will send you to Kettle to sign in. Enter the code Kettle emails you, then press Allow. That is the whole setup. In Claude, this is under Settings, then Connectors. In ChatGPT, search for Kettle in Apps once it is listed there.

What you can ask

  • “How is Mom’s day going?”
  • “What did we write about Dad this week?”
  • “Who can I call if I can’t reach Mom?”
  • “Add a note: called Mom, she sounded great.”
  • “Reply to Anita’s note: I’ll bring dinner Saturday.”

What it can and can’t do

It can read your parent’s day, the record for any one day, your family’s notes, the who-to-call list, and which circles you belong to. It can add a note and reply to a note, and it reads a new note back to you before saving it.

It can’t change settings, pause Kettle, add or remove a parent or a family member, or see anything outside your circles. Those stay in the Kettle app.

Turning it off

Open the Family tab in Kettle and press Disconnect next to the assistant. The connection ends at once, and the assistant keeps nothing from Kettle after that except whatever it already showed you in a conversation.

Privacy

What an assistant can read and add is described in the privacy page, under “Assistants you connect”. What the assistant does with what it reads is governed by that assistant’s own privacy policy.
