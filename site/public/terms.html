LINKABIT AI LABS LLC (“HeyKettle”, “Kettle”, “we”) operates this service. These are the terms in plain words.

What Kettle is

Kettle passes along how a parent’s morning started. An adult child sets it up, and the parent agrees to it during setup. Kettle sends the family a short note about the day, and when a morning is not as usual it asks the parent first.

What Kettle is not

Kettle is not an emergency service and not a medical service. It does not replace calling, visiting, or emergency help. If you believe someone is in danger, call your local emergency number.

Your account

You sign in with your email address. Only add a parent who has agreed to be part of Kettle. You are responsible for the people and phone numbers you add.

Your parent’s standing

Your parent can turn Kettle off at any time, without permission and without telling anyone. Replying STOP to any text ends the texts. Message and data rates may apply.

Doing our best

We work to make sure messages arrive, but phones, carriers, and networks fail sometimes. Kettle is provided as is, and a quiet phone is never a conclusion about anyone.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we will say so plainly on this page with the date.
