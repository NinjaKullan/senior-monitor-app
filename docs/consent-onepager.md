# What this is, and exactly what it collects

*For: Appa. From: Hema. July 2026. One page. Strike anything you don't like — whatever you strike, I remove.*

## What it is

A tripwire, not a camera. Your phone tells my server one thing: **"a normal thing happened"** — for example, you opened WhatsApp. That's it. The point is that I stop needing to wonder, and you stop getting worried calls. If your morning is quiet, the system will one day ask *you* first, quietly, before anyone else is told — but during this 30-day trial, nothing contacts you or anyone else at all. Only I see the data.

## Exactly what is sent (the complete list)

| When | What my server records |
|---|---|
| You open WhatsApp | `dad, whatsapp, <date & time>` |
| You open YouTube | `dad, youtube, <date & time>` |
| You open the news app | `dad, news, <date & time>` |
| (Optional) phone put on charge | `dad, charging, <date & time>` |
| Steps | Daily step count, via Apple's own Health Sharing, phone to phone |

Three fields per event: who, which routine, when. Nothing else is transmitted, and the server is built to discard anything beyond those fields.

## What is never collected — not "not stored," never collected

- No message content, contacts, or who you talk to
- No call logs, no browsing history, no what-you-watched
- No location, no GPS, no travel history
- No microphone, no camera, no photos
- No typing, no passwords, no reading anything on your screen

The mechanism can't see these even in principle: it's an Apple Shortcuts automation you can open and read yourself — it fires when an app opens and sends one web request. There is no app of mine installed on your phone.

## Your controls

1. **See everything:** a private webpage lists every ping your phone has ever sent. You can check it any time, forever.
2. **Turn it off yourself:** Shortcuts app → Automation → delete. No permission needed, no notice to me required. I will never reinstall it without asking you first.
3. **Withdrawal is total:** say the word and I delete your entire history the same day.
4. **If you ever can't decide for yourself** (illness, hospital): the default is it comes off, and Amma + I decide together whether it ever goes back on.

## The data

Stored on a private server only I control, under a password. Never sold, never shared, never used for anything but "did Appa's normal morning happen." Deleted in full at the end of the trial unless we both agree to continue.

## Why I'm asking you and not just Amma

Because you'll read this like a lawyer, and whatever you strike out becomes the rule — not just for you, for the product. If this design can't earn your signature, it doesn't deserve anyone's.
