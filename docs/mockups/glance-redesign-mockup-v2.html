What changed, and why

Every screen answers a question first. Today answers “is everyone okay?” in one line before anything else. The parent page answers “do I need to do anything?” in a literal box. Details exist, but they are optional reading.

No mental math. Relative time leads everywhere (“Heard from 12 minutes ago”), with both clocks as the quiet second line. The words are “heard from”, never “checked in”, because Mom does nothing.

Worry becomes action. The Call button sits on the card, one tap from the feeling to the phone.

The day is a shape, the week is dots. The arc replaces three prose rows; seven dots replace five sentences. The dots reuse the three hearth states and carry no tally, so one odd day is information, not a scoreboard.

Family notes are the family’s memory. Plain text, a name, a date when one matters, links that work. Future dates surface in Upcoming. Nothing here asks anything of Mom, and nothing turns into a chore.
