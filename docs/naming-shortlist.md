# Naming Shortlist — ranked top 10 and pick

*July 26, 2026. Brief: warm/calm/dignified, never medical or surveillance-adjacent, works across diaspora corridors without being culture-specific, sayable by a 40-year-old in North Carolina and a 75-year-old parent, spellable on hearing, plausible .com/.care/.family. Collision checks are rough web screens, not counsel opinions — screen the finalist properly before committing.*

## Ranked top 10

**1. Lamplight**
Story: the small evening light that shows a home is awake — visible from far away, and it never looks back.
Mouths: LAMP-light; trivial for both; "lamp" is borrowed or cognate in Tamil, Hindi, Tagalog, Spanish (lámpara), Mandarin-learner English alike.
Vibe check: a lamp emits, it doesn't record — the attorney has nothing to bristle at; lighting the evening lamp is a real ritual in Indian, Filipino, Mexican, and Chinese homes without the name naming any of them.
TM/domain: moderate-low — Lamplight Farms/TIKI (torch fuel, distant class), a HK social-analytics SaaS, small agencies; nothing found in care/family tech. lamplight.com likely held; lamplight.care / lamplight.family / getlamplight.com plausible.
Sentence: "Lamplight says Amma's day started normally." — reads as warmth, not telemetry.

## The pick: Lamplight

It is the only candidate that carries the product's exact physics — a small light that shows the home is awake, visible across any distance, that never looks back — and it passes every test at once: both mouths, no surveillance residue for an attorney to find, a ritual every target corridor recognizes without the name borrowing any single culture, and the least trademark drag of the warm candidates. Kettle names the sensor's ancestor and belongs to the founder's story (keep it as the repo codename and the YC origin anecdote); Lamplight names what the buyer feels — the light on, an ocean away — and the buyer is the child. The tagline writes itself ("The lamp is on"), the alert inversion stays gentle (the lamp isn't lit yet), and "Lamplight says Amma's day started normally" sounds like something a family would forward to each other. One copy caution: "we'll leave the light on for you" is Motel 6's famous slogan — the sentiment is adjacent, so never use that phrase verbatim.

## Lanes mined and closed (so they aren't re-derived)

Sunrise → Sunrise Senior Living. Lantern → end-of-life planning startup. Kindred → Kindred Healthcare. Cadence → Cadence (RPM for chronic care). Aura → Aura digital frames, the exact diaspora-gift aisle. Nest → Google. Ember → the mug. Allwell/Allswell → Centene Medicare brand / Walmart mattress. Homefires — the idiom is perfect and the word "fire" fails the bad-moment test. Vigil, Beacon, Sentry, Lighthouse, Whistle, Guardian — the entire watching/alarm class is banned by the brief and rightly so. The "all is well in translation" lane (Shanti, Sukoon, Anshin, Ping An, Mabuhay, Tutto Bene) closes by construction: each is culture-specific, which the brief forbids — and Ping An is one of the largest insurers on earth. Human and bird names (Jasmine, Wren, Robin) fail the sentence test by turning the daily line into a stranger's report — "Jasmine says Amma is fine" invents a persona, and a persona is three steps from a robot the senior must answer to.
